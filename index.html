Research Focus

I specialize in spatial transcriptomics and multimodal integration of high-dimensional tissue data. My work centers on aligning and quantifying transcriptomic and proteomic signals across histological structures using AI-driven segmentation and image analysis. I'm particularly interested in understanding how the peripheral nervous system remodels in pain pathology.
